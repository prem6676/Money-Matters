# FINANCIAL CALCULATIONS

Do not hardcode calculated values.

Calculate dynamically.

For example:

Total Income =
sum of all income transactions

Total Expenses =
sum of all expense transactions

Balance =
Total Income - Total Expenses

Savings =
Income - Expenses

Category spending =
sum of expense transactions grouped by category

Dashboard statistics must automatically update when transactions change.
